# rolling_onn
A cozy winter sushi café! Open index.html, tap Start, and roll sushi step by step: pick a plate, season and stir the rice, add fillings, roll, slice and plate it up. 🥄 Tap anywhere once to wake the soft music (♪ mutes). Finale: giggly cats and party poppers! 🎉🐱 Tap to pop more. Made with love and snow 💗
