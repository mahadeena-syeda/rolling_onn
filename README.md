🍣 Rolling On ❄️
a cozy little winter sushi café, made just for you ♡

Welcome in, welcome in! Shake the snow off your boots, grab a warm seat by the frosty window, and let's roll some sushi together~ 🌨️✨

Everything is soft, slow, and squishy-satisfying. There's no timer and no way to lose. It's just you, a wooden bowl of rice, and two very happy cats. 🐱🐱

🌸 How to play

Open index.html in any browser. It's one cozy file, so there's nothing to install! 🥰 Tap Start making sushi and follow your heart (and the little hints at the top).

Step	What you do 🍙
1️⃣ Plate	Pick your favourite pastel plate: rose moon, mint kitty, lilac snowpal or butter square 🎀
2️⃣ Roller	Choose a bamboo roller in round, heart or square 🎍
3️⃣ Nori	Untie the seaweed and smooth it out flat 🌿
4️⃣ Season	Sprinkle salt, pepper, chili, oil or soy. Listen to them fall! 🧂
5️⃣ Stir	Watch the wooden spoon stir the rice all by itself. Tap to pause 🥄
6️⃣ Scoop	Drag three spoonfuls of rice onto the nori 🍚
7️⃣ Spread	Smooth the rice from the middle outward 🧈
8️⃣ Menu	Choose your fillings 📜
9️⃣ Fillings	Lay them down the middle, nice and neat 🥒
🔟 Roll	Drag upward on the mat to roll it up 🌀
1️⃣1️⃣ Slice	Slice carefully along the dashed lines 🔪
1️⃣2️⃣ Plate up	Add your condiments 🌶️
🎉 Party!	The cats laugh and the party poppers go POP!
🎧 Sounds
🎶 Background music: leberch-soft-soft-music, tucked right inside the page so it travels with you. It starts on your very first tap, so tap anywhere to wake it up!
🔇 Mute button: the little ♪ in the top-right corner. Tap it to hush everything (it turns into a ×).
🥢 Little sounds: rice grains clicking, wooden spoon swishes, sprinkles, soy drops and chimes.

🎧 Headphones make the rice-stirring sounds extra squishy and soothing!

🎊 The grand finale

On the last page:

😹 both cats laugh, bounce and wave their paws
🎉 two party poppers pop confetti by themselves, and you can tap anywhere to pop them again
✨ forty little stars twinkle all around
🔁 tap Make another roll to start fresh
🧸 Good to know
📱 It works on phones, tablets and computers. Swipe or use the Back / Next buttons at the bottom.
🔒 Next stays sleepy until you finish each step. That's how the game guides you along!
🌐 It's all in one file, so it works offline too.
🎵 If the music is silent, tap once anywhere on the page. Browsers like to wait for a little tap before playing sound.
